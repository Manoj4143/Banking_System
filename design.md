## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, ultra-premium neo-fintech narrative. Combining institutional security with razor-sharp consumer dynamism, it pairs the deep, grounding confidence of dark forest emerald with high-contrast electric lime and warm architectural off-whites.

The visual style merges tactile neo-fintech minimalism with subtle physical metaphors and precision graphics:
- **Atmosphere**: Confident, agile, discerning, and frictionless.
- **Visual Stance**: Deep emerald foundations anchored by obsidian framing, illuminated with punchy kinetic chartreuse accents and hairline geometric alignments.
- **Tactile Quality**: Smooth pill geometries, refined frosted glassy overlays, and whisper-quiet depth built through atmospheric ambient shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate tension between established wealth management and modern decentralized agility:

- **Primary (`#064E3B`)**: Deep Forest Emerald. Anchor for dense typographic hierarchies, hero card surfaces, secure state badges, and authoritative actions.
- **Secondary (`#B8FF24`)**: Electric Lime / Kinetic Chartreuse. High-energy functional beacon reserved for conversion drivers, positive yield vectors, dynamic active pills, and inline editorial highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`)**: Vibrant Deep Teal. Bridges primary emerald and bright lime; applied to secondary data charts, multi-currency wallets, and transactional indicators.
- **Neutral Dark (`#12161A`)**: Obsidian Charcoal. Used for deep contrast preview shields, physical card representations, and primary header typography.
- **Canvas & Surface**: Soft warm off-white canvas (`#F4F5F0`) paired with pristine crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) card planes to prevent sterile digital glare while maintaining high optical contrast.

## Typography

Typography balances bold, rhythmic display presence with clinical tabular legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Tight negative letter-spacing with hefty weights (700, 800) delivers an editorial punch. Key words and phrases utilize inline high-voltage pill containers or lime text highlights to command attention across transaction metrics and onboarding slogans.
- **Interface & Metrics (`Inter`)**: Neutral, highly legible workhorse for tabular figures, operational microcopy, ledger balance sheets, and transaction notes.
- **Tabular Numerics**: All numeric outputs (balances, currency conversions, APY displays) utilize monospaced figure settings (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure zero visual jitter during streaming live ticker feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column responsive architecture designed to handle complex financial dashboards, ledger streams, and card preview configurations:

- **Canvas & Grid Framework**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, max-width 1280px, 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`), 48px outer margins (`margin-lg`).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, 24px gutters (`gutter`), 24px outer margins (`margin`).
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, 16px gutters (`gutter-sm`), 16px outer margins (`margin-sm`). Reflows card stacks and multi-currency wallets to vertical scrolls with edge-to-edge swipe containers.

- **Rhythm & Safe Areas**:
  - Generous padding inside containers maintains breathing room around dense balance metrics.
  - Spacing internally scales predictably using 8px-derived tokens (`space-sm`, `space-md`, `space-lg`, `space-xl`), keeping data tables dense while allowing visual cards and banner actions sufficient isolation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle translucent layers, ambient tinting, and micro-hairlines:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Warm off-white (`#F4F5F0`), un-elevated base layer.
  - **Level 1 (Cards & Standard Surfaces)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px hairline border (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.06)`) and ultra-diffused drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.06)`.
  - **Level 2 (Floating Controls & Popovers)**: Pure white or frosted obsidian (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.92)` backdrop blur: 16px) with an ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Floating Action Bars)**: Crisp white with deep directional diffusion: `0 32px 64px -16px rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.16)`.

- **Atmospheric Accents**:
  - Subdued, layered orbital line patterns rendered in hairline strokes (`1px solid rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`) run through backdrops to evoke precision radar, cryptography, and navigational guidance without cluttering data.

## Shapes

The geometry draws from fluid neo-fintech physical tokens, featuring prominent pill containers and softened architectural card boundaries:

- **Base Curvature**: Level 3 roundedness drives extreme pill radii on interactive inputs (`border-radius: 9999px`) and generous smooth corners on structural cards (`rounded-lg: 2rem` to `rounded-xl: 3rem`).
- **Interactive Controls**: Buttons, currency chips, status tags, and action pills embrace complete capsule form factors.
- **Containers & Device Frames**: Surface cards and preview mockups utilize continuous curve squircles (24px to 32px), creating a natural synergy between physical device viewports and virtual cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Kinetic Lime)**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`). Background `#B8FF24`, label in `#12161A` (weight 700). Subtle active press scale down (`0.98`). Hover introduces a 2px offset border glow.
- **Secondary Action (Forest Solid)**: Background `#064E3B`, label `#FFFFFF`. Smooth hover transition to `#0B634C`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action**: Transparent pill, 1px hairline border (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.1)`), text `#12161A`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Account Card**: White canvas, 24px-32px rounded corners, hairline boundary (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.06)`). Inner content spaced with `space-lg`.
- **Obsidian Dark Card (Black Tier / Virtual Card Preview)**: Background `#12161A`, text `#FFFFFF`, accent details in `#B8FF24`. Features holographic metallic sheen overlay using diagonal linear gradients.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Yield Indicators**: Pill-shaped micro-containers (`roundedness: 3`).
  - Active/Positive: Lime-tinted surface (`rgba(184, 255, 36, 0.18)`), text `#064E3B`, with a 6px solid `#064E3B` pulse dot.
  - Neutral Category: Soft grey-white, text `#12161A`, hairline outline.

### Input Fields
- **Amount & Transfer Fields**: Pill or large soft-squircle enclosing boxes. Border is 1px hairline expanding to 2px `#064E3B` on active focus. 
- Large-denomination currency inputs drop internal containment borders, displaying oversized tabular numbers directly on card surfaces with dynamic trailing currency selector chips.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: 20px rounded squircle (6px radius). When checked, filled with `#064E3B` displaying a crisp white micro checkmark.
- **Radios**: Concentric pill-circle design. Active state features an outer `#064E3B` ring and an inner `#B8FF24` kinetic center dot.

### Lists & Transaction Feeds
- **Row Elements**: Edge-to-edge separation with hairline dividers (`rgba(18, 22, 26, 0.05)`).
- **Merchant Avatars**: 44px circular or squircle containers with light neutral fill (`#F4F5F0`).
- **Amounts**: Tabular numeric Inter; positive gains rendered in `#064E3B` with leading `+`, debits in `#12161A`.
